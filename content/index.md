## About Me


I'm someone who is deeply curious about the world and has an almost instinctive need to understand things rather than simply accept them at face value. I enjoy learning across different subjects, following questions wherever they lead, and connecting ideas that might initially seem unrelated.

My interests move across finance, accounting, business, technology, physics, psychology, history, philosophy, books, and the countless smaller subjects that happen to catch my attention. I don't necessarily want to become an expert in everything I encounter. More often, I simply want to understand things well enough to form my own perspective and know where that understanding can take me next.

I'm currently pursuing ACCA, which has given me a structured foundation in accounting, finance, taxation, auditing, and the broader world of business. But formal education is only one part of how I learn. A significant amount of what interests me comes from reading, experimenting, researching, observing, and going down completely unexpected rabbit holes.

This website is an attempt to give all of that learning somewhere to live.

I created it as a personal space for collecting ideas, notes, observations, lessons, questions, and things I don't want to forget. Some of the things here may be polished and carefully thought through; others may simply be fragments of an idea that I am still trying to understand. Not everything needs to become a finished article. Sometimes a note is valuable simply because it captures where my thinking was at a particular moment.

You'll find my ACCA notes, things I've learned, highlights and thoughts from the books I read, entries from my life, and whatever else I eventually decide belongs here.

I also want this website to preserve the process of learning, not just the results. Knowledge is rarely acquired in a straight line. There are questions, wrong turns, forgotten ideas, sudden connections, periods of intense curiosity, and long stretches where something quietly sits in the back of your mind before finally making sense.

So this is less of a portfolio and more of a record of that process.

A place to learn, think, collect, revisit, and occasionally change my mind.

And because the most interesting part of learning is often realizing how much there is still left to learn, this website will probably never really be finished.
