#### SKANS Notes Question 1 and 2

On April 20X5, Nova Ltd purchased specialized machinery for $5 million. The machinery will be used for five years, after which the company must dismantle the machinery and restore the site. 
The estimated decommissioning cost is $2 million, payable in five years time. Nova Ltd uses a discount rate of 8%, and the discount factor for five years at 8% is 0.681. 
The machinery is depreciated on a straight-line basis over its useful life with no residual value. 

**Required:** 

**a) What amount should be recognized as the initial cost of the machinery on 1 April 20X5?**

**b) What is the total charge to the statement of profit and loss relating to machinery and decommissioning obligation and for the year ended 31 March 20X6?**

**Answer:**

Purchase Price = $5,000,000
Decommissioning cost at PV = $2,000,000 x 0.681 = $1,362,000
Asset initial cost = $6,362,000 **(a)**

Debit - PPE $6,362,000
Credit - Cash $5,000,000
Credit - Liability $1,362,000

PPE Depreciation = $6,362,000 / 5 = $1,272,400
Unwinding of discount (Interest) = $1,362,000 x 8% = $108,960

**SPL Charge (b)**

| Item             | Category           | Amount           |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| Depreciation     | Operating Category | $(1,272,400)     |
| Interest Expense | Financing Category | $(108,960)       |
| **Total Charge** |                    | **$(1,381,360)** |

#### SKANS Notes Question 3

On 1 January 20X6, Jigar Chemicals opened a hazardous waste treatment facility. The facility is expected to operate for four years until 31 December 20X9, at which point it will be dismantled and the site restored in accordance with environmental regulations. At the commencement date, the estimated cost of decommissioning the facility was $6 million, payable in four years' time. Jigar Chemicals considers a discount rate of 10% to be appropriate, and the discount factor for four years at 10% is 0.683.

**What is the total charge to the statement of profit and loss in respect of the decommissioning obligation for the year ended 21 December 20X6.**

**Answer:** 

Decommissioning cost at PV = $6,000,000 x 0.683 = 4,098,000

Debit - PPE $4,098,000
Credit - Liability $4,098,000

Unwinding of Discount (Interest) = $4,098,000 x 10% = $409,800
PPE Depreciation = $4,098,000 / 4 = $1,024,500

**SPL Charge:**

| Item                  | Category           | Amount           |
| --------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| Depreciation          | Operating Category | $(1,024,500)     |
| Unwinding of Discount | Financing Category | $(409,800)       |
| **Total Charge**      |                    | **$(1,434,300)** |

#### Test Your Understanding 2 (Kaplan Study Text)

On 1 April 20X8, the fair value of Xu's property was $100,000 with a remaining life of 20 years. Xu's policy is to revalue its property at each year end. At 31 March 20X9, the property was valued at $86,000. The balance on the revaluation surplus at 1 April 20X8 was $20,000 which relates entirely to the property. Xu does not make a transfer to realized profit in respect of excess depreciation. 

**Required:** 

**a) Prepare the extracts of Xu's financial statements for the year ended 31 March 20X9 reflecting the above information.**

**b) State how the accounting would be different if the opening revaluation surplus did not exist.** 

**Answer:** 

**W1:**
Depreciation = $100,000 / 20 = $5000 /-year
Carrying Value at year end = $100,000 - $5000 = $95,000

**W2:**
Revaluation at year end = $86,000
Revaluation Loss = $95,000 - $86,000 = $9,000

**a) Preparing Extracts for the year ended 31 March 20X9:**

**Statement of Profit and Loss and Other Comprehensive Income**

| Item                | Category  | Amount  |
| ------------------- | --------- | ------- |
| Depreciation W1     | Operating | $(5000) |
| Revaluation Loss W2 | OCI       | $(9000) |
**Statement of Financial Position**

| Non-Current Assets  |                             |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- |
| PPE                 | $86,000                     |
| **Equity**          |                             |
| Revaluation Surplus | $11,000 ( $20,000 - $9000 ) |

**Statement of Changes in Equity**

|                 | Revaluation Surplus |
| --------------- | ------------------- |
| Opening Balance | $20,000             |
| W2              | $(9000)             |
| Closing Balance | $11,000             |

**b)** If the opening revaluation surplus did not exist, then the revaluation loss of $9000 would need to be taken through the statement of profit and loss as an impairment expense.
