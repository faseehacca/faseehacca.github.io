 Fundamental Principles of ACCA's Code of Ethics
 5 fundamental principles
-Integrity - Honest and straightforward. Don't distort facts or manipulate facts. 
-Objectivity - Free from influence. Auditor needs to be independent. 
-Professional Competence and Due Care - Auditor needs to have skills require to practice audit (professional competence) and keeping skills up to date (attending CPD sessions) (due care)
-Confidentiality - Auditors come across lots of confidential information. Its his duty to ensure that this info is not disclosed to any party outside of audit and it is not used for personal advantage. However under some conditions, confidentiality is breached, e.g. when the auditor comes across fraudulent transactions that indicate money laundering or something, but only after consulting their legal counsels.
-Professional Behavior - Auditors must avoid any actions that discredits the profession.

5 Ethical Threats
-Self Interest
-Self Review
-Familiarity
-Advocacy
-Intimidation

Self Interest Threat
The threat that a financial or other interest will inappropriately influence the professional accountant's judgement or behavior.
